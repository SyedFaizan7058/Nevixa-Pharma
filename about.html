Our Story

A pharma company built around three questions

Before any Nevixa formulation reaches a pharmacy shelf, it has to answer three questions: is it made better than what came before it, does it hold up to consistent quality checks, and can our partners depend on it being available when they need it.

That discipline is what shaped Nevixa Pharma — a healthcare-driven company based in Navi Mumbai, working across four focused divisions to serve doctors, pharmacists and distributors across India.
