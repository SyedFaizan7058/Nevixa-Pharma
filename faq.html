About Nevixa Pharma

Nevixa Pharma specializes in pharmaceutical formulations across four divisions — Derma, Cosmo, Pedia and General — built on innovation, quality and commitment.

We serve dermatology, cosmetic care, pediatric health and general medicine, offering formulations tailored to each segment's specific needs.

Every product is measured against the same three principles: innovation in formulation science, consistent quality across every batch, and long-term commitment to our partners and patients.
