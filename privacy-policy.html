1. Introduction

Nevixa Pharma ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through nevixapharma.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — when you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, subject and message.
  • Automatically collected information — through Google Analytics, we may collect your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited to understand how visitors use our site.
  • Communication information — if you contact us via phone, email or WhatsApp, we retain the details of that conversation to respond to your inquiry.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to product, partnership and distributor inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • Understand website traffic and improve our content and user experience
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of our website
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses Google Analytics, which places cookies in your browser to help us understand site usage. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; note that some site features may not function as intended if cookies are disabled.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website relies on the following third-party services, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic and usage analytics
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery
  • Google Maps — embedded map on our Contact page
  • Web3Forms — processes and delivers contact form submissions to our team
  • WhatsApp — used for direct messaging via our floating contact button
  • Bootstrap, Font Awesome and AOS (CDN-hosted libraries) — used for site styling, icons and animations

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties for details on their own data practices.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your personal information with third parties except where necessary to operate our website (such as our form-processing provider), to comply with a legal obligation, or with your consent.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights & Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you've shared with us by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our Pedia division products are intended to be used by or under the supervision of parents, caregivers or healthcare professionals.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
